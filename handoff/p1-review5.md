## Must fix

1. **A share can keep capturing after the main big screen disappears.** [session.ts](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/hub/src/session.ts:993) always counts `"main"` as a visible display, even when its last report has expired. `removeScreen("main")` also returns without removing it ([session.ts](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/hub/src/session.ts:1115)), and the Mac app sends close notifications only for extra screens ([main.rs](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/app/src-tauri/src/main.rs:269)). The share sweep therefore never starts its off-screen deadline for a share shown on the closed main window. Count `main` only while it has an active display report, notify the hub when that window closes, and test close and report expiry with a live share.

2. **Invisible shipped work can still become an agent directive.** [brain.ts](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/hub/src/brain/brain.ts:923) accepts `seen` when just two content words overlap the shipped update, then creates `coordinate` directives. I ran the shipped text “Shipped background sync status telemetry to the metrics collector” through `parityCandidate`; it passed. The invented `seen` value “sync status badge in toolbar” also passed `grounded`. This leaves the fourth finding from review 4 unresolved despite the new check, and contradicts [SPEC.md](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/SPEC.md:521). Require an explicit user-visible change supplied by the shipping agent and validate `seen` against that field before issuing parity directives. Add this invisible-shipment case to the tests.

3. **A synchronous canvas bridge error bypasses recovery.** [canvasJoin.ts](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/web/src/shared/canvasJoin.ts:58) calls `fc.execute` outside its rejection handler. A throwing bridge makes `poll()` reject; [ui.tsx](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/web/src/shared/ui.tsx:978) starts `tick()` without handling that rejection. I reproduced the rejected poll with a throwing `execute`. Repeated ticks can produce repeated unhandled rejections without ever marking the join failed or scheduling a rejoin. Treat a synchronous throw as a missed poll and test that repeated throws enter the retry path.

## Design concern

- **Distinct features can suppress one another.** The ten-minute dedupe in [brain.ts](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/hub/src/brain/brain.ts:892) and [brain.ts](/Users/cheyrasmussen/jamsesh-p1/jamsesh/hub/src/brain/brain.ts:913) divides shared terms by the *smaller* term set. A short feature name wholly contained in a longer, distinct one scores `1` and can discard the second shipment. Deduplicate on a stable feature identity or require substantial overlap in both directions.
